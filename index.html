Zuspan Regimen Loading Dose

• 4g IV: Give 20 mL of 20% MgSO4 solution IV slowly over 5-10 minutes.

• 10g IM: Give 10 mL of 50% MgSO4 solution deep IM into each buttock (5g per side) mixed with 1 mL 2% Lidocaine.

Maintenance Regimen

• 5g IM (50% solution): Administer every 4 hours into alternate buttocks for 24 hours post-delivery or post-last seizure.

Toxicity Monitoring (Prior to each IM dose)

1. Patellar reflex must be PRESENT.

2. Respiratory rate must be >= 16 breaths/min.

3. Urine output >= 30 mL/hr over preceding 4 hours.

Antidote: Calcium Gluconate 1g IV (10 mL 10% solution) slowly over 3 minutes if respiratory depression occurs.
